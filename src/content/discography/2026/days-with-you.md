With this release, I broke a promise with my past-self. "*What's that promise?*" you may ask, well I think it doesn't matter anymore ~

It's about some unknown feelings I've been getting lately. So to overcome those, I started writing a small diary entry named "Days with You" (yosh, me love writing!). But overtime, instead of getting better, it kind of got painful. So I stopped writing and you can say it's unfinished. And even after that, at some point when it got hard to bear and burst out with so many thoughts and stuff.. that's when I wrote this song "Days with You". I'm not sure how well I was able to describe my feelings, but you may get idea what's going on after listening (hopefully not?).
