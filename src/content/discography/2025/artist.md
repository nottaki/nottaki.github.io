So my parents & relatives often ask me questions like "*Ah-.. so you make music? How much money have you earned from it? Are you famous? How many people listen to your music?*". And I'll be like, "*No one really listens to my music. And I'm not even famous. Money!? What money??*". Then they say, "*Oh, I see... so it's just a hobby!*". It really makes me angry and sad at the same time.

Then, (if me remember correctly) on March 12, 2025 at 09:35 PM, one of my relatives called me out and said, "*I'm free right now! So, can you show me that music thing you do and how you do it?*".. It was like a "little performance" for me in front of my relative, right? So me opened FL Studio, and felt nervous thinking: "*What do I make? What do I show to them?*". It's not like I can make something out of blue if not feeling inspired or something. But yeah, I did make something, saved the project as "performance", and they were like, "*Hm... okay! But I don't understand this kind of music*". I never thought I'll ever open this project again. But you believe in coincidences or not, later that year, I happened to listen to "Musician" by "[Porter Robinson](https://porterrobinson.com)" (again). And while the whole song resonated with me (just like everyone else), the line "How do you do music? Well, it's easy..." - hit really hard. It reminded me of that "little performance" I had. It reminded of all the burn outs. The whole song made me question a lot of things. I stupidly even felt anger/jealous toward him thinking: How do you even make music? I can't do it all the time and just end up staring at the blank project. Ah, yes you did say "Well, it's easy...", but I just don't get it! What do you mean? How is it easy? Oh, maybe it's "talent" or something that's natural to you and everyone else and they were born with it.. maybe I don't have any of it. And that's where the "performance" became "Artist".

P.S.: The worst thing you can say to someone, especially artists, is "You have talent". It is like saying "You have got it in easy mode and you don't have to work hard, you got it naturally" - completely throwing away the hard-work, sleepless nights they've put to get to this point. Just my thoughts ~
